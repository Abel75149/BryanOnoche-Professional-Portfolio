# 🛡️ PwC Forage Cybersecurity Simulation – Threat Response & Risk Mitigation

## 🔍 Overview
This virtual simulation, offered through **Forage in partnership with PwC**, presented a realistic cybersecurity scenario involving internal threats and potential data breaches. The project mimicked consulting workflows, requiring me to analyze risks, assess impact, and communicate findings to non-technical stakeholders.

---

## 🧪 Project Scope
- Analyze reported internal data security incidents
- Classify threat types and possible vectors
- Conduct a basic risk assessment and scoring exercise
- Create a professional report outlining risk mitigation strategies
- Communicate complex findings in simple, business-friendly language

---

## 📄 Deliverables
- 📝 Incident response summary
- 📊 Risk matrix and business impact analysis
- 🛡️ Recommendations for improving technical and procedural safeguards
- 🗣️ Stakeholder communication write-up for internal escalation

---

## 🎯 Skills Applied
- Cyber risk analysis  
- Threat modeling (insider threats, data exfiltration)  
- Business impact communication  
- Report writing and executive summaries
